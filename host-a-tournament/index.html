Host a tournament

Below you’ll find links to everything you need to host Fermi Poker, from the rules to the question set.

Materials

  • The guide

    How to play, the rules to read out, an ante schedule and variations.

  • Rule sheet (PDF)

    All the rules on a single page. Print a copy or two for every table.

  • 63 questions to print (PDF)

    Question, hint and answer cards, and blank cards for your own questions.

  • The cards on a screen

    Show the questions, hints and answers on a phone, tablet or laptop instead of printing them.

Hosting a tournament at a school or university? It’s free, and we may even pay for things like poker chips and small prizes. Fill out our contact form. Planning a tournament at your company? Write us at contact@fermi.org.

Anything else you’d like to know? Write to contact@fermi.org
