September 29, 2026 marked the 125th birthday of Enrico Fermi. To celebrate his life and work, we’re planning to sponsor hundreds of Fermi Poker tournaments at schools and universities.

Fermi Poker is an estimation game that combines the betting mechanics of poker with Fermi estimation. Instead of being dealt random cards, you make guesses about questions like “How many new smartphones were sold worldwide last year?” You write down a secret guess, bet on it, get helpful hints, bet again, and win by either having the best guess or by bluffing everyone else out.

Hosting a tournament at a school or university? It’s free, and we may even pay for things like poker chips, dry-erase markers and small prizes. Fill out our contact form. Playing with friends? Our guide covers everything you need. Planning a tournament at your company? Write us at contact@fermi.org.
