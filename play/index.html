Fermi Poker

    Rules

    Before you start

    Who acts first?

    Randomly pick who acts first during the betting rounds. For the next question, the player on their left goes first, and so on.

    How many chips does everyone get?

    Everyone starts with the same amount of chips. A good default is to give each player chips worth 1,000, with the ante starting at 25.

    What’s the ante?

    Everyone puts in the ante, a small mandatory bet. Then the question is revealed. It goes up over time, so you can’t wait forever for a question you’re sure about.

    How fast does the ante go up?

    The organizer announces the ante and how often it goes up, for example every 20 minutes. If you want to use a fixed ante schedule, here’s a good default for players starting with chips worth 1,000:

    Can we use a calculator or look things up?

    Don’t use the internet. Calculators are out too, unless your group agrees to allow them.

    What if someone has seen the question before?

    Just skip to the next question, with Skip this question in the ⋯ menu.

    Your guess

    How do I write my guess?

    Write it secretly and place it face down. Write numbers however you like, for example 3,000,000, 3 × 106, 3 million or 3M, as long as they’re clearly written and not ambiguous.

    Does a range include its endpoints?

    A range includes both of its endpoints.

    Can I change my guess?

    Once betting round 1 starts, you can’t change it.

    How long do we have to guess?

    There’s no limit. If someone keeps everyone waiting, try a 60-second clock: once half the players have written down their guesses, the rest have 60 seconds. Use a phone timer or a one-minute sand timer.

    Betting

    What can I do on my turn?

    Play moves clockwise. On your turn you can:

    Check
    Pass the action to the next player without betting. Only possible if nobody has bet yet in this betting round.
    Bet or raise
    Increase the highest bet. Others must match it to stay in. The minimum bet is the current ante, and a raise must be at least the size of the last bet or raise in this betting round.
    Call
    Match the highest bet so far.
    Fold
    Drop out of this question. You give up the chips you’ve put in, but risk nothing more.
    All-in
    Bet all your chips. This is possible even if what you have left is less than the highest bet.
    What’s the minimum bet or raise?

    The minimum bet is the current ante, and a raise must be at least the size of the last bet or raise in this betting round.

    When does a betting round end?

    A betting round ends once everyone has acted and matched the highest bet or folded.

    Not enough chips left for the ante?

    You’re automatically all-in.

    How does all-in work?

    If you’re all-in, you can win from each player only as much as you put in yourself. Extra bets go into a side pot that only the players who paid into it can win.

    Say Bob bets 200, Carol calls, and Alice goes all-in with the 100 she has left. The main pot gets 100 from each of the three, and whoever has the best range wins it. The other 100 that Bob and Carol each bet go into a side pot, which can only go to Bob or Carol, but not Alice.

    Who wins the pot

    Who wins?

    At the showdown, the remaining players reveal their ranges. The narrowest range that contains the answer wins. Width is the high endpoint minus the low one: if the answer is 500, a range of “200–600” (400) beats a range of “400–1100” (700).

    Equally narrow?

    Split the pot.

    No range contains the answer?

    The range whose midpoint (halfway between low and high) is closest to the answer wins.

    Everyone else folded?

    The last remaining player wins and doesn’t have to reveal their guess.

    Everyone, or all but one, is all-in?

    Since no more betting is possible, read the remaining hints and answer. Then go to the showdown.

    Do I have to show my range if I folded?

    Revealing is voluntary for everyone who folded.

    End of the game

    What if I run out of chips?

    A player with no chips left is out, but can stay seated for a while and keep guessing for fun, just without betting.

    When does the game end?

    The game ends when one player has all the chips. If you have a fixed end time, you can instead stop on time, and whoever has the most chips wins.
