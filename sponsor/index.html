Sponsor Fermi Poker tournaments

To honor Enrico Fermi on his 125th birthday, we're helping students run Fermi Poker tournaments at schools and universities, with hundreds of events planned this year. Sponsors make them possible.

What your support pays for

Chips and question cards

Poker chips for every table, and printed question cards.

Pizza and drinks

Food for the players, like at a hackathon.

Prizes

Prize pools for the winners.

Why it matters

Fermi Poker trains estimation, calibration and updating on evidence, and it's social: every question gives the table something concrete to argue about. More under What players learn.

Get in touch

Interested in sponsoring tournaments? Write to daniel@fermi.org.
