Support Fermi Poker tournaments

With your help, we can bring Fermi Poker to hundreds, and later thousands, of schools and universities. Every tournament needs things like poker chips and dry-erase markers, and ideally snacks, soft drinks and small prizes. That’s what your money pays for.

Interested in becoming a sponsor or making a donation? Write us at contact@fermi.org.
